Display-for-soul, sans-for-sense. Playfair Display for the tender moments — names, the item being decided — only at display sizes. Inter carries everything functional; body is 17px for unhurried, 40+-friendly reading. Headings are neutral ink, never colour.

03 · Principles

Named rules

Brand never speaks Danger

Safety hues are reserved for their conventional meanings. The expressive palette never poaches them, and they’re never decorative.

Colour is never the sole signal

Buckets and states pair hue with a label, icon, or position — never hue alone. Colour-blind safe by construction.

One spark

Marigold is the only bright accent and appears rarely — the single most-loved item. Warmth otherwise comes from soft violet and gentle copy.

One family, two lights

Every value descends from #004A67 / #005667. Sea Glass rises to white by day; Sea Glass at Night is the same spine inverted toward black, lifting by glow.

Elevation by light, not mud

In dark, never separate planes with a muddy mid-tone fill. Text sits on a dark surface (AAA); a luminous rim + glow carries the lift.

Restraint is not minimalism

Aesthetic, not minimalist. Restraint governs noise and load, never beauty — never drain colour or warmth “to look cleaner.”

Do
  • +Frame the work as affirmation — “which do you love more?” — never as culling.
  • +Keep one decision in view; let airy whitespace carry the calm.
  • +Let the photographs be the richest thing on screen.
  • +Verify AA in both themes; support ≥44px tap targets.
Don’t
  • –Make this a catalog, inventory tool, or shopping site.
  • –Gamify letting go: no confetti, streaks, points, or “you have too many.”
  • –Let the brand speak danger, or distinguish states by hue alone.
  • –Colour a heading, use Playfair small, or separate dark planes with mud.
