Reading them
All three of your picks are lighter than the current #8E7EA8 — they read as the gentlest wash. A (#B5AACC) still carries enough colour to host white-ish text and frame a hero; B and C are nearly tints — lovely as soft section backgrounds, but too pale to fill a hero halo with punch or to sit behind dark text as a chip. If decor will be used freely (fills, framing, chips), I’d lock A as the decor token and treat B/C as optional lighter steps of the same violet. Tell me which.
