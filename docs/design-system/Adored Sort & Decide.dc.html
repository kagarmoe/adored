Adored — sort & decide (round model)
A loop of short, timed rounds against a ranking that firms up over time. Three moods (Triage required; Compare & Useful optional). The goal is to find your adored things, not rank all 100. You return to the Decide hub between rounds.
The loop
Hub recommends a round → you pick (or accept) → timed round → ends on timer or empty queue → “what moved” → back to hub. Moods you choose, never a forced march.
Quiet ring, no clock
The round timer is a depleting violet arc — no digits (a number ticking down adds pressure). It empties as you go, turns gold when done. Triage = deliberate taps; Useful = swipe reflex.

How the moods relate
  • Triage sorts by feeling (required).
  • Compare ranks your top two bins (Love & Like) by binary insertion — a few high-value choices per item, not all-pairs. The timer just bounds the round, not the task; the ranking carries across rounds.
  • Useful sub-orders within Love: loved-and-used sits above loved-but-not. “No” nudges down a notch — it never leaves Love.
