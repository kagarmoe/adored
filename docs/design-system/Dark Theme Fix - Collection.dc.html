✗ Card melts into the page · ✗ heavy teal footer slab · ✗ dim grey text
What changed (globals.css / component CSS)
1 · Cards separate by a rim, not a shadow. Drop-shadows vanish on a dark ground. Give every card border:1px solid var(--color-hairline) + a faint inset top highlight — that's the “elevation by light” rule. The big workspace card needs an outer border too.
2 · Footer is not a slab. Use the ground colour with a 1px top hairline + muted text — never a saturated fill bar.
3 · Lift dim text. Secondary/links were near-invisible; ink-soft → #AEBFC4, links use the bright primary #7FD4E2.
4 · “Add photos” as an outline button needs a visible border (#4F9FB0), not a faint one.
Root cause
The palette is right — the app is just rendering dark cards with a box-shadow for elevation (which is invisible on near-black) and no border, so every layer collapses together. Dark mode must lift by light: a visible hairline rim + a slightly raised surface + a soft glow on the active panel.
