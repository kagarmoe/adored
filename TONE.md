# Tone

> v2, 2026-10-01. The original contract is archived at
> [`docs/archive/TONE-v1-2026-10-01.md`](docs/archive/TONE-v1-2026-10-01.md). This version is a
> guide, not a contract: it describes the feel; `DESIGN.md` picks the mechanisms; good design
> judgement fills the gaps.

## The feel

Warm, calm, familiar. Like the resale apps people already use, with the sales pressure removed and
some coziness added. The person is looking at their own things and deciding what they love.

## Voice

- Speak from the person's side ("your bag", "what you love"), not the system's ("record updated").
- Short, plain, kind. No exclamation marks doing the work of warmth.
- Ask forward ("Where will it go next?"), not backward ("Why does this matter to you?"), at the
  moment of choosing.
- Never count against the person: no "too many", no "5 still undecided", no streaks.

## Surface

- Photographs lead. Color is welcome; chrome stays quieter than the objects.
- Familiar patterns beat clever ones: cards, carousels, detail panels, filters, overflow menus.
- One decision in focus at a time, even when a grid is on screen.
- Motion is soft and optional (reduced motion honored). Nothing bounces for attention.
- Destructive actions are sober and reversible where possible; red only for the irreversible.

## Still off the table

- Gamification: confetti, points, streaks, scores-as-reward.
- Shame or urgency on routine actions.
- Shopping language inside the app: buy, cart, offer, price.

When in doubt, choose the option that a careful friend helping you sort your closet would choose.
